FORMATS

Industry standards, opened directly

.stl

Binary and ASCII. The 3D-printing standard.

.obj +.mtl

With materials and colors — pick both files together and colors apply instantly.

.3mf

The modern additive-manufacturing format, with colors.

.step .stp

Precise engineering geometry. Converted on-device, no internet.

.iges .brep

The same CAD kernel as STEP. Surface models open alongside solids.

.ply

The 3D-scanner format, with color on every vertex.

.zip

Unpacks itself. Multiple models inside — pick one or open them all together.

TOOLS

Big-CAD tools — pocket sized

Smart Snap measuring

Distances, face angles, radius and diameter from three points, wall thickness in one tap. Snaps to vertices.

Weight, area, cost

Part mass: PLA, PETG, ABS, nylon — plus metals from steel to 14K/18K gold. Alongside it, surface area and cost at your own material price.

Section analysis

Cut along X/Y/Z or perpendicular to your line of sight, with a hatched cross-section, CAD-style. Look inside the part from any angle.

Assembly breakdown

Pull the parts apart with a slider — even if the model arrived as a single STL. The app finds separate bodies where the format stores none.

6 display modes

Rendered, Shaded, Wireframe, X-ray, Ghosted, Technical — with gold, silver, chrome, copper, glass, and diamond materials. Any single part can get its own color and material.

CAD-grade controls

Navigation cube, ortho/perspective, lighting joystick, Zen mode on double tap.

Save as STL, OBJ, 3MF

What you see is what gets saved: one part, an assembly without the clutter, or several files merged into one — with colors, where the format keeps them. Also works as a STEP-to-STL converter.

Hide a part

Press and hold a part, or use the list — hide it, or isolate it and hide everything else. Handy for weighing or measuring one sub-assembly on its own.

Parts list

The whole assembly by name, like a CAD tree. Names come from the file — STEP, OBJ, 3MF. The list sits in a sheet along the bottom, so the model stays visible and under your control.

Project

The whole job on a model in one file: orientation, colors, hidden parts, the section cut, the exploded view and the camera are saved together with the model itself. Send a project on or open it on another device — a heavy STEP assembly then loads in a fraction of a second.

Video capture & snapshot

The explode and the spin record to video with a whole number of turns, so the clip loops seamlessly. It captures the model itself — no buttons, no phone status bar; a PNG frame is a separate menu item.

Library

Point the app at a folder of models and it shows them with thumbnails and sub-folders, with no twelve-file limit on recents. A neighbouring .mtl is picked up automatically. Free.

Scale

The model came in inches, or it doesn't fit the print bed — resize it here: by X/Y/Z dimension, by presets (×2, inches↔mm), or from a segment you already measured. A single part, too.
